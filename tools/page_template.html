Track record

How it works: twelve factors, from recent form and venue record to key player form and the captain's and coach's track record, are each scored from −5 to +5 toward one team and multiplied by a weight. The total goes through a logistic curve, so no side ever reaches 0% or 100%. Tests also carry a draw chance. Claude locks in its pick when a match starts, then records the result, so the track record shows how the method is doing. This is a form guide for fun, not betting advice.
